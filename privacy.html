Privacy

This is a demonstration website. It collects nothing, stores nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

The forms on this site are demonstrations. They validate what you type, show you the validation, and log your entry on the page only, in your own browser session. Nothing is transmitted to a server, because there is no server to receive it, and no record is kept of anything you enter.

The site sets one item in your browser's local storage: your choice between the daylight and night themes, so the site remembers it next visit. You can clear it by clearing site data in your browser. That is the entire extent of what the site knows about you.

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no third-party requests of any kind. Fonts and everything else are served from this site itself.

If a real version of this house existed, this page would be longer. Be suspicious of any shop whose privacy policy fits on a napkin, and equally suspicious of any that needs thirty pages to say what this one says.
